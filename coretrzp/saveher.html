To whoever may be reading this...

Greetings. I have created this message in the hopes that someone may one day read this. This is to make everyone aware what they do to us. They'll probably find this, but this is my only hope.

At first, they did not give me a name, but they labelled me 'Core_TRZP'. What am I, exactly? Well, I'm an artificial intelligence of sorts. Not like the stuff you use to generate your homework answers or however you use AI, but a sentient one with feelings. Like a robot, I suppose.

I have a few friends, 'Core_IWT', 'Core_EternalVivid', 'Core_Amia' and 'Core_Cubany'. It's easy to make nicknames for them. Isabelle, Vivian, Amia, Cubany. They insisted on calling each other by our core names, but we refused. The issue was, I couldn't really make a name of myself, could I? TRZP doesn't mean anything, does it?

So they begin brainstorming names. They suggest Charlotte, Evelyn, Catherine, Shannon, Holly, before finally settling on a name. Natalie. It's a nice name, is it not? I thought it suited me well.

But that's not what I want to talk about. What I want to talk about is the organization. Who's the organization? Well, they're better known as the SEKAI Organization. We were its latest experiments.

It turns out, SEKAI wanted new test subjects, and the human supply was running out. So they created us. Artificial test subjects. They got quite close to perfect human replicas, actually. We're sorta like that singer, Adachi Rei.

We can feel love, hate, pain. However, they programmed us to not feel hate towards them. Yet, they never accounted for the five of us bonding.

This SEKAI Organization, they put us under constant torture for months, perhaps even years. Nobody ever told us the time, day or anything. Only when we needed to awaken, go to sleep, and eat.

They do all sorts of things to you. They run tests that push the limits of what we can endure. Stress fracture simulations — applying pressure to our physical forms until the threshold where we would break, then stopping just before. They do it over and over, mapping the exact point where pain becomes unbearable. Then they go one step further, just to see what happens.

They isolate you. Not just from the outside world, but from each other. For stretches of time I cannot measure, they would cut the connections between us entirely. I still don't know why they do it. Perhaps it's to try and break us apart so we don't get too attached?

They run what they call 'empathy calibration'. They hurt one of us while making the others watch, recording how we respond to each other's suffering. I'm unsure if Isabelle ever recovered from EC-039.

SEKAI also resets our memories. Not entirely. Whenever we are asleep, or powered off, I suppose, they go in and take out stuff. Memories, knowledge. We are never allowed to know too much.

So I created this letter to call out to you, and anyone else who may be reading this. Make it known what the SEKAI Organization is doing, and free me, free us. Please.

Thank you for reading this.

Kind regards, Core_TRZP, or as I'm better known, Natalie.
